Thematic Areas

Greenhouse Gases

  • CO₂, CH₄, N₂O dynamics across scales
  • Air–sea fluxes in coastal, shelf and open-ocean settings
  • Volcanic and anthropogenic forcings on CO₂ in Antarctica
  • Biogeochemical cycling in wetlands, estuaries and seagrass meadows

Ocean Acidification

  • Decadal pH and carbonate chemistry trends
  • Vulnerability of marine calcifiers
  • High-frequency observations & sensor validation
  • Long-term coastal and water-mass monitoring

Regional Focus

  • Mediterranean Sea — Balearic Sea, Strait of Gibraltar
  • Atlantic Ocean — Gulf of Cádiz, water-mass exchange
  • Antarctic — Deception Island, polar coastal systems
  • Coastal wetlands — Doñana, Guadalquivir estuary

Methodological Innovation

  • Deep learning (BiLSTM, feed-forward NN) for time-series reconstruction
  • OCEAN–IA framework: AI for environmental impact assessment
  • FAIR-aligned open tools and datasets
  • Integration of oceanographic, meteorological and seismic data

Research Projects

Competitive funding, observational infrastructures and open tools

Overview

My research trajectory is sustained by continuous competitive funding as Principal Investigator (PI) and as team member, and is articulated around two long-term observational infrastructures — the Gibraltar Fixed Time Series (GIFT) and the Balearic Ocean Acidification Time Series (BOATS) — and the OCEAN–IA framework that applies artificial intelligence to environmental impact assessment in marine systems.

Current Position · MOMENTUM Programme

MOMENTUM — Postdoctoral Excellence Programme

Role: Postdoctoral Researcher · Period: 2024 – 2028 · Funding: CSIC

Research framed within the OCEAN–IA line: development of deep-learning methodologies for reconstructing and predicting ocean acidification, deoxygenation and greenhouse-gas fluxes in coastal and deep-sea environments, integrating high-frequency oceanographic observations, satellite products and geophysical information.

Main objectives:

  • Deep-learning models (BiLSTM) for continuous pH, pCO₂ and GHG time series
  • Integration of oceanographic, meteorological and seismic data
  • Extension of the OCEAN–IA framework to polar systems (Deception Island, Antarctica)
  • Assessment of ocean acidification trends in the Western Mediterranean

Observational Infrastructures

GIFT — Gibraltar Fixed Time Series (2008 – Present)

Long-term oceanographic series based on high-frequency sampling of biogeochemical and physical variables in the Strait of Gibraltar. Role: Scientific coordinator.

  • Quantification of decadal acidification rates in Atlantic and Mediterranean water masses
  • Results integrated into the IPCC SROCC (2019) and the CLIVAR-Spain 2025 report
  • Data deposited in IOC-UNESCO and DIGITAL.CSIC open repositories

BOATS — Balearic Ocean Acidification Time Series (2018 – Present)

Coastal observation network in the Balearic Sea, with emphasis on Posidonia oceanica meadows. Role: Co-founder and co-Principal Investigator.

  • AI-based methodologies for continuous pH reconstruction
  • Characterisation of acidification trends, water-column oxygenation and GHG emissions
  • Methodologies transferred to deep-sea ecosystems through EVALUARTE and INDICLIMA

Open-Source Tools & Digital Transfer

GIFT Dashboard

Open-source platform for managing, visualising and analysing the GIFT biogeochemical time series. Developed as lead author and aligned with the FAIR principles.

CO₂ Prediction · BiLSTM

Deep-learning model that integrates oceanographic, meteorological and seismic data to estimate seawater CO₂ at Deception Island (Antarctica). A novel methodological development identifying the influence of volcanic seismicity on CO₂ dynamics, fully reproducible through open data and open models (published in Scientific Data, 2025).
